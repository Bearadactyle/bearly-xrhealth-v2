About BEARLY.xrhealth

A Personal Case Study by Bearadactyle

BEARLY.xrhealth was designed by Bearadactyle as a personal case study and logging system to navigate my own health journey. Living with chronic health challenges requires consistency, low-friction tracking, and clear personal baselines over time.

While built to serve my specific daily logging needs, I wanted this to be a tool that anyone can use to empower themselves in their own personal health journeys.

My passion is driven by wanting to help people get a good, clear scope on where their health stands without paywalls, subscription fees, or locked features. This is a completely free, local-first, privacy-focused open source project designed to be accessible on smartphones, desktops, smart glasses, and XR headsets.
